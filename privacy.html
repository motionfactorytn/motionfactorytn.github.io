Privacy Policy

Crazy Frogs (Android app com.motionfactory.crazyfrogs, web version and Discord Activity) · by Motion Factory · Last updated: November 23, 2026 · See also our Terms of Service

This policy explains what information Crazy Frogs collects, why, and the choices you have. You can play the whole game as a guest without giving us any personal information.

1. Information we collect

  • If you play as a guest: your progress, coins and outfits are saved only on your device. We receive nothing.
  • If you sign in with Google: we receive your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use them to create your account and to save your progress (levels, best times, coins, outfits, daily gift and streak) in the cloud so it follows you to other devices.
  • Leaderboard: when you set a record on a level, your name, profile picture and time are shown publicly to other players on that level's leaderboard. Your email is never shown.
  • Online play with friends: to connect players in a room, the game uses a peer-to-peer connection (PeerJS). Your chosen frog, outfit and in-game moves are sent to the other players in the room. Like any internet connection, this exchanges IP addresses between the devices in the room.

2. Advertising (Android app)

The Android app shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos in the shop and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about your device and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent first and can choose non-personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Services we use

  • Google Sign-In – signing in (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase – stores accounts, cloud saves and the leaderboard (Supabase Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob – ads in the Android app
  • PeerJS – connecting players for online rooms
  • Discord – when you play the Discord Activity, the game runs inside Discord and its network traffic goes through Discord's proxy. The game does not ask for or receive your Discord account information; you play as a guest and progress is saved on your device (Discord Privacy Policy)

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use analytics or tracking beyond what is listed here.

4. How long we keep it

Your account and cloud save are kept while your account exists. Leaderboard records stay for the current season; old seasons may be archived. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

5. Children

Crazy Frogs is a casual game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

6. Delete your account and data

You can stop using your account at any time by choosing Play as Guest in the game. To have your account, cloud save and leaderboard records permanently deleted, send us a message with the name shown in your game account on:

  • Facebook: Motion Factory page
  • Discord: our server

We delete the data within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Each player can only read and change their own save; leaderboard times are checked on the server.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact Motion Factory on Facebook or Discord.
